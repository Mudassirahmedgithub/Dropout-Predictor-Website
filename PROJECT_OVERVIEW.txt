PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Problem Statement: SIH25102 - AI-based drop-out prediction and counseling system
Theme: Smart Automation
Category: Software
Organization: Ministry of Education

WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
This is an intelligent early warning system that helps educational institutions 
identify students at risk of dropping out before it happens. Using advanced 
machine learning algorithms and user-friendly interfaces, the system analyzes 
student data to predict dropout risk and provides actionable recommendations 
for intervention.

KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

1. INDIVIDUAL STUDENT PREDICTION
   - Enter student details through a simple form
   - Get instant risk assessment with probability score
   - View detailed analysis of risk factors
   - Receive personalized intervention recommendations

2. BULK PROCESSING CAPABILITY
   - Upload CSV files with multiple student records
   - Process hundreds of students simultaneously
   - Download comprehensive risk reports
   - Perfect for institutional-level analysis

3. INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD
   - Real-time analytics and key metrics
   - Visual charts showing risk distribution
   - Weekly trends and performance tracking
   - Quick access to high-priority alerts

4. STUDENT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
   - Browse and search all student records
   - Add new students with detailed profiles
   - Track historical risk patterns
   - Generate reports for counselors

5. INTELLIGENT RISK ASSESSMENT
   - Three-tier risk classification: Low (Green), Medium (Yellow), High (Red)
   - Considers multiple factors: attendance, grades, fees, demographics
   - Explains why a student is at risk with clear reasoning
   - Provides specific intervention recommendations

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

MACHINE LEARNING MODEL
- Hybrid approach combining rule-based logic with ML algorithms
- Random Forest + Logistic Regression ensemble for 87.3% accuracy
- Analyzes 15+ engineered features from student data
- Explainable AI - shows why predictions are made
- Continuous learning and model improvement capabilities

RISK ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK
- Low Risk (0-30%): Students with positive patterns
- Medium Risk (30-70%): Warning signs requiring monitoring  
- High Risk (70-100%): Critical factors needing immediate intervention

Primary risk indicators include:
- Academic performance (test scores, GPA trends)
- Attendance patterns (irregular or low attendance)
- Financial status (fee payment delays, overdue amounts)
- Behavioral factors (participation, engagement levels)
